Think and Grow Rich

A ledger of thirteen principles for turning desire into achievement.

Success, Hill argues, begins with a clear purpose, unwavering belief, persistent action, and the willingness to learn from failure. Though framed around wealth, the same ledger applies to careers, craft, and any goal worth keeping.

    The Book in One Sentence

    Clear goals, belief, consistent action, persistence, and continuous learning combine to give you a greater chance of success.

    A note on the science: some ideas in the book — autosuggestion, the "sixth sense" — are motivational concepts rather than scientifically established principles. The most widely supported practical takeaways are setting clear goals, planning, building useful skills, staying persistent, and taking consistent action.
